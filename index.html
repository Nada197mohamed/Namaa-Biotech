About Namaa Biotech

Namaa Biotech is an educational platform created to bridge the gap between academic biotechnology education and real laboratory and career experience.

01

Our Mission

We aim to make biotechnology knowledge more practical, accessible and connected to the real world, helping students understand what comes after graduation.

02

What We Offer

Practical courses, laboratory concepts, training materials and career-oriented guidance designed around the realities of biotechnology.

03

Our Vision

To help biotechnology students and graduates build confidence, understand laboratory workflows and make more informed career decisions.

🧬
ABOUT ME

Dr. Nada Mohamed

Biotechnologist • Science Teacher

I am Nada Mohamed, a Biotechnologist and Science Teacher at an international school, as well as a Science teacher for Arabic-speaking students. Through my academic and practical experience, I have developed a strong interest in connecting biotechnology education with real laboratory practice.

I created Namaa Biotech to share practical knowledge, raise awareness about the real experiences and opportunities available to biotechnology graduates, and help students understand how to move from university knowledge into laboratory and career reality.
